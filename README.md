# Red Run Rally

A family kart race around the neighborhood. Drive three laps and trap the other karts in bubbles.

Play: https://mach1rcode.github.io/Red-Run-Rally/

- Three tracks: Home, Troy, and White Lake.
- Seven family drivers. One player or two players on a split screen.
- Three race levels: Easy, Normal, and Fast. A half speed option and an auto gas option help small children.
- Keyboard or touch controls. The sound can be turned off.

## Add to an iPhone Home Screen

1. Open the link above in Safari.
2. Tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".

The game is one file, `index.html`. It needs no build step.
